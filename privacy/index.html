Privacy Policy

This site is a one-person homepage. It has no accounts, no forms and no tracking scripts. Here is exactly what that means.

The short version

I collect nothing about you on this site. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party embeds that build a profile of you. The only personal data I ever hold is what you choose to send me in an email.

Who is responsible

Pikujs Solutions, operated by Nirmaan J Sarkar, India. Contact: pikujs@gmail.com.

What is collected automatically

Like every website, the server that delivers these pages receives your request. That includes your IP address, the page you asked for, the time, and your browser's user-agent string. This is standard web-server logging, and it exists so the site can be served and abuse can be stopped.

WhatWho sees itHow long
Requests, IP addresses, user agentThe hosting provider and me, in server logsShort-lived, per the host's retention policy
Font requests (Google Fonts)Google, which receives your IP address to serve the font filesGoverned by Google's own policy

I do not combine log data with identity, and I do not use it to build profiles.

Cookies and local storage

No cookies are set. The site uses your browser's local storage for one thing only: remembering which colour palette you picked. It never leaves your device and it contains no identifier.

Analytics

None. There is no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no pixel, no session recorder. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

When you email me

If you send a message to pikujs@gmail.com, I receive your address and whatever you write. I use it to reply and to run the project if we work together. I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not sell or share it. Email lives in Google Workspace, so Google processes it on my behalf.

Links to other sites

Pages here link out to GitHub, GitLab, LinkedIn and project demos. Once you follow a link, that site's privacy policy applies, not this one.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and for visitors in the EU/UK under the GDPR, you can ask what personal data of yours I hold, ask for a copy, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be erased. Email me and I will answer within 30 days. If the only data is an email thread, erasure means deleting the thread.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and adults. I do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page rather than announced somewhere else.
